# One Line, Zero Use: The Useless Superpower Sprint

One original line, 134 characters, describing a hilariously useless superpower: seeing five seconds into the future — just long enough to watch yourself drop your phone and do absolutely nothing about it.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
